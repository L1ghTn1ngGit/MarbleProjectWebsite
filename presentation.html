      How to read these numbers

      Working budget is the adopted budget plus in-year adjustments and transfers. Spent is the cash expense recorded for the same line. Only Department of Education lines are analyzed, and lines with negative working budgets or cash spending are excluded. A difference is cash spent minus working budget; it is not evidence of misconduct. Dollars are nominal.
