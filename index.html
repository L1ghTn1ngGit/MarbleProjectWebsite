How these numbers are calculated
  • Records: the source data has 43,249 budget lines, and only Department of Education lines are included. Of those, all are analyzed; lines with a negative budget or spending amount are left out.
  • Budget is the working budget (the adopted budget plus in-year adjustments and transfers). Spent is the cash expense recorded for the same line.
  • Over budget by 10%+ counts lines where cash spent is more than 110% of the working budget. Lines with a $0 working budget are not counted, because no percentage can be calculated.
  • In the table, Over Budget means any amount over, Overrun means more than 50% over, and On Track means spending equals the budget.
  • Source rows are retained as supplied, including identical rows; the CSV has no unique record ID for safe deduplication. Counts are budget lines, not separate programs or projects. One program can appear on several lines and in several years. Figures are reported amounts and do not by themselves show why spending differed from the budget.
